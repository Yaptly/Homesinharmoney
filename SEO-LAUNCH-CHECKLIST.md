# Homes In Harmony local SEO launch checklist

## Google Business Profile
- Use the exact business name: Homes In Harmony LLC.
- Choose the most specific primary cleaning-service category available.
- Configure the business as a service-area business unless customers can visit a staffed location.
- Add Morgantown, Cheat Lake, Westover, Star City, Suncrest, Brookhaven, Granville, and Fairmont only if they remain actively served.
- Use 304-491-5175 and https://homesinharmonyllc.com consistently.
- Add genuine team and completed-work photos.
- Add each actual service and a concise description.
- Ask customers for honest Google reviews; never gate or incentivize positive reviews.
- Respond to every review in the business's natural voice.

## Google Search Console
- Verify the domain property for homesinharmonyllc.com.
- Submit https://homesinharmonyllc.com/sitemap.xml.
- Request indexing for the homepage, services page, four service pages, and service-area page.
- Review Pages, Core Web Vitals, and Enhancements after deployment.
- Track queries containing Morgantown, cleaning, deep cleaning, move-out cleaning, and commercial cleaning.

## Accuracy checks before merge
- Confirm licensed and insured remains accurate.
- Confirm all staff are background checked.
- Write the exact satisfaction-guarantee terms and publish them in the site terms.
- Add a real social-sharing image when authentic brand photography is available.
- Add privacy and service/cancellation terms before collecting significant customer traffic.
